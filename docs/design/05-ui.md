# 05 — UI

All interface is HTML/CSS built in `src/ui/ui.ts` (styles in `src/ui/style.css`), with no framework, images or web fonts. Headings use a system serif; body text uses a rounded system sans. Colours: parchment `#F3E3C3`, shade `#E2CDA4`, ink `#2B2235`, leather `#7A4A2B`, wax `#C0392B`, stamp red `#D7322E`, gold `#E8B23A`, pencil `#8A8478`.

## Layout (phone-first)

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Top-left | **Log Pose**: a glass bubble whose needle points along the routed direction. It quivers while the target is over the horizon. A label underneath reads "SANJI · BARATIE" or "VIA THE NORTH STAIR". |
| Top-centre | **Zone banner**: a paper strip "REGION · NAME". Stamped paper **toasts** queue underneath (about 2.2 s each). |
| Top-right | **Log Book** (a leather notebook with a red wax-seal dot when something is new) and the **Den Den Mushi**. The snail is an inline SVG that sleeps with "Zzz" offline, rings, and tints its face with the giver's colours. Tapping it opens the Log Book. |
| Bottom-right | Contextual **TALK** button, **emote** wheel, **sea chest** (outfits), **jump** (touch only). |
| Bottom-left | **Touch stick** (`src/ui/stick.ts`): it appears where a finger lands in the left half and outputs −1..1. |
| Bottom-centre | **Dialogue box**: a coloured name tab, a typewriter line, and ▼ when the line is done. Tap, click, Space or E advances it. |

## Panels

- **Log Book**, with three tabs:
  - **JOBS** has one page per open job: title, step text, carried item, and tap to track. Below that are the DELIVERED stamps and who is waiting.
  - **CHART** (`src/ui/chart.ts`) paints Nami's equirectangular chart. Unvisited zones are pencil "?" rings and inked zones get labels. Laugh Tale is a permanent fog smudge. It also shows the you-are-here dot, the target star, the five stamps and Zoro's sticky note.
  - **SETTINGS** covers music and SFX volume, blips, quality, large text, reduced motion, online, night sky, RESET (with a two-step confirm) and CREDITS.
- **Sea chest**: four slots of four options. Locked options show "?" with a hint.
- **Emote wheel**: ten emotes, with locked ones shown as "?".
- **Wanted-poster card**: on first meeting a Straw Hat, a non-blocking card slides in for 2.5 s with a portrait crop of the live frame.
- **Offer prompt**: two buttons, for example A NEWS COO OFFERS A LIFT [FLY] [NO THANKS].
- **End screen**: a CONGRATULATIONS stamp and a WANTED · FOR SERVICES TO THE CREW poster with the KANPAI frame, bounty, stats line, CONTINUE and CREDITS.

## Accessibility

Everything has aria-labels, focus-visible rings and aria-live toasts and dialogue. Escape closes panels. `body.large-text` and `prefers-reduced-motion` are supported. All dynamic text is escaped.
