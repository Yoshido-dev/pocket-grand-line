# Island-by-island exploration and character pass

This pass follows the user's request to visit and improve every island individually. Each entry records a browser visit, an authored layout change, a follow-up visual review, and practical access checks. Zone moves, story spots, chests and transport anchors are guarded separately. The source baseline lives in `tools/qa/island-anchors-before.json`; the live anchor baseline is `tools/qa/island-runtime-before.json`.

## Shared support

Automatic dressing now leaves authored paths, named interaction spots and chest contact areas clear. Authors can reserve additional walks or clearings with `reservePath`. Random flora collision radii now use the same scale as their rendered geometry. Large double-sided canvas sails and flags use the same camera cutaway as solid props; this fixes the Sunny's sail obscuring the courier and Nami.

Authored trails have a continuous surface clipped directly onto the rendered ground triangles. This is now the sole path renderer: the old vertex-colour painting and its `gPath` preparation were removed, preventing wide colour bleed across coarse triangles while reducing runtime work and bundle size. Rounded joins, dry-ground clipping and interpolated ground normals share one merged mesh with no outlines or colliders. All five geometry fixtures in `tools/qa/paths.test.mjs` pass; the shared browser review confirmed trails on Coo Beach, Sunny Dock and Cocoyasi, then checked Syrup Village and Loguetown before and after removing the old painting.

The shadow normal bias in `src/game/game.ts` increased from 0.06 to 0.12 to remove PCF self-shadow stripes. Visual comparisons covered 0.08, 0.10 and 0.15 as well as medium (512) and high (1024) shadow maps; ground contacts were reviewed, and the existing -0.0008 depth bias remains unchanged.

Duplicate prop helpers now share the Sunny implementations through their existing exports: Paradise aliases `put`, `heading`, `crate`, `bench` and `toonMesh`, while New World aliases `put` as `at` and `toonMesh` as `toonM`. New World's frame wrapper uses the unchanged Paradise R50 frame and retains its latitude and longitude. A fixed-source build comparison measured a 632-byte gzip saving with no frame changes; the 124 then-installed exploration cases also passed after consolidation.

Obsolete hairstyle generators were removed: the shared hair helper retains the used crop style, and Luffy's unused `mane` generator was deleted. The disabled line-of-sight sentinel in materials/game changed from z=-100000 to +100000, placing it behind the camera so the `pd > 0` cutaway branch stays off in title, eagle and frozen views. Whole Cake cloud close-ups confirmed the tiny unwanted centre holes are gone; follow-camera gameplay retains its normal cutaway.
